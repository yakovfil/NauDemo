click.wav is an original generated 100 ms decaying sine click, authored for this
demo and distributed under the repository's BSD-3-Clause license. It contains
mono PCM16 at 48 kHz. Its deterministic waveform uses amplitude 9000,
exponential decay exp(-sample/650), and phase
2*pi*(1800*sample/48000 + 600*(sample/48000)^2).
# Battlezone effects

`fire.wav` and `explosion.wav` are newly synthesized PCM16 mono 48 kHz effects:
a decaying pitch sweep/noise shot and a longer decaying low-frequency noise burst.
They contain no original arcade recordings. They follow the repository BSD-3-Clause license.
